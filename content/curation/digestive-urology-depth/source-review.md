## Evidence and permitted reuse

The twelve source pages are original federal NIDDK educational prose, a foundation source under the repository sourcing policy. NIDDK attribution, specific HTTPS URLs and retrieval dates appear in every entry. Images, video, third-party figures and the publications listed in NIDDK's bibliographies were not imported or used as source text. A bibliography's link to a proprietary publication does not mean that publication was retrieved or reproduced.

All twelve sources were retrieved directly from NIDDK on September 17, 2026, after primary-source web discovery. The source-snapshots directory contains extracted text and JSON retrieval records with requested/resolved URLs, retrieval date, fetched-HTML hash and retained-text hash. Raw HTML is not retained. The extractor includes clinical warning callouts, including “Seek care right away”; an initial extraction that omitted some callouts was replaced before authoring. All retained text hashes match the manifest. An access date does not make an older page a newly published guideline.

## Boundaries and source defects

- The ERCP source groups endoscopic ultrasound with tests that do not enter the body. That inaccurate classification is omitted; MRCP and ultrasound are the examples used. No procedural success or numerical complication estimate is imported.
- Cystoscopy aftercare mentions reporting persistent symptoms after a day while a later callout uses two days for painful urination. The guide uses the earlier reporting advice and immediate reporting for severe pain, severe bleeding, inability to void, fever or clots. Stent discomfort is distinguished from these findings. There is no fixed stent-removal date.
- Uroflowmetry is described as measuring voided volume and flow, not measuring the volume still in the bladder. Postvoid residual is explained separately. The source's single numerical residual cutoff is not used as a universal diagnosis or catheterization trigger.
- Urinary imaging does not reproduce the older source's broad contrast-nephrotoxicity assertion, a fixed fluid/fasting regimen or routine antibiotic duration. No contrast-clearance advice or kidney-protection protocol is supplied.
- Prostate Tests is used only for PSA interpretation limits. Its wording that PSA with a rectal examination “rules out” cancer, routine age-based rectal-exam schedule, and prostate-massage instructions are excluded.
- The liver biopsy and retention symptom pages were last reviewed in 2019; their dates are visible in attribution. No transfusion threshold, coagulation cutoff, mandatory positioning duration or observation/discharge interval is supplied. Local procedural instructions remain necessary.
- Scope is adult nursing education. There are no patient-specific doses, automatic anticoagulant holds, pediatric protocols, catheter insertion/irrigation steps, stent removal instructions or independent procedure selection.
