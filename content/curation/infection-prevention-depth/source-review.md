## Checked distinctions

- C. difficile: symptoms versus colonization; molecular, antigen and toxin assay limits; prompt stool handling; at least 48 hours of Contact Precautions after diarrhea resolves; no test of cure. CDC's routine-care preference for alcohol sanitizer when hands are not visibly soiled is distinct from additional soap-and-water precautions during outbreaks. Gloves and correct removal remain central.
- C. auris: colonization still transmits; correct species identification and screening are facility/public-health functions; acute-care versus nursing-home precautions; EPA List P disinfection; shared-device responsibilities; no clearance from a negative swab or a claimed decolonization regimen.
- Norovirus: fluid-loss consequences; early outbreak controls; whole-stool and RT-qPCR testing; soap and water after care during outbreaks; splash-appropriate PPE; at least 48 hours after symptom resolution for outbreak precautions and staff exclusion.
- Measles: prompt isolation and notification; fit-tested respirator even with presumptive immunity; rash day zero and routine four-day isolation, with immunocompromised exceptions; ventilation-based clearance; occupational-health monitoring and exposure-specific restrictions.
- Shingles: localized, coverable lesions in an immunocompetent patient differ from disseminated disease or possible dissemination in an immunocompromised patient; lesion PCR is preferred; staff illness and exposure are different occupational-health pathways.
- Influenza: older adults may be afebrile; rapid antigen and molecular tests are different; inpatient precautions use seven days after onset or 24 hours after fever and respiratory symptoms resolve, whichever is longer; aerosol-generating procedures need additional precautions; novel influenza A guidance is separate.

The targeted legacy edits correct blanket handwashing and distance-only droplet-mask wording without relabeling the entirety of the older entries as newly reviewed. Earlier procedures and source locators outside these exact edits remain in the wider library review queue.
