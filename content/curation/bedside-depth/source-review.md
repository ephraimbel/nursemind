## Corrections and exclusions

- CVC: identify PICCs as central lines; remove a blanket claim that they have lower infection risk; replace universal flush/lock volumes with device-specific requirements.
- CLABSI: distinguish adult site-selection guidance from advanced-CKD dialysis-access preservation; apply the 2017 adult short-term non-tunneled CVC chlorhexidine-dressing update; distinguish gauze, transparent, blood/lipid and propofol change intervals.
- CAUTI: a disconnected, leaking or aseptically compromised system requires replacement of catheter and collecting system under CDC guidance, not merely disinfecting and reconnecting it. Distinguish port culture sampling from special nonculture bag collection.
- Chest drainage: distinguish water-seal bubbling from wet-suction bubbling; remove universal output thresholds and improvised disconnection/dislodgement rescue instructions. Keep assessment, urgent escalation and manufacturer/facility procedure boundaries explicit.
- Wounds: retain detailed assessment and routine ordered care without copying restricted staging tools or implying that all eschar should be debrided.
- Transfusion: retain initial observation, patient/component verification and stop/new-tubing response; exclude source temperature-conversion errors, inappropriate leukocyte-filter generalization and universal rate/time rules.
- Feeding tubes: initial radiographic confirmation for blindly inserted tubes and ongoing migration checks; no air-auscultation shortcut or residual-only assurance of safety. Medicine formulation, water, feed holds and suction intervals require the individual plan.
- Neurological assessment: observations and changes are described without reproducing a proprietary score or the source's erroneous GCS upper/lower-bound wording.
