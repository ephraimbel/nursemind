# Cross-category source review — September 17, 2026

Thirteen new entries and three revised existing entries cover drugs, drips, labs, procedures, diagnoses, scenarios, communication and general references. The manifest maps 151 authored/revised blocks to 22 primary sources and retains source text, retrieval metadata, four versioned SPL XML files and final Swift hashes. The two respiratory references retain their earlier sections; their manifest claim maps cover the ten added blocks. The UPCR/UACR entry is replaced in full while retaining its identifier and enum registration.

## Retained evidence and exclusions

FDA label excerpts preserve substantive label text; table rows are reformatted and any summary is identified as such. Drug nursing-implication and patient-teaching fields are omitted where no additional nursing source was used. Empty uncitable quick-reference rows were not filled with clinical claims. Manufacturer images and promotional material are excluded.

Federal NLM, NIH, FDA and AHRQ original text is the foundation for non-drug content. NLM's third-party bibliography is not an authoring source; no StatPearls, A.D.A.M., AHFS, Testing.com or proprietary nursing material was adapted. The AHRQ pages were retrieved through web extraction after direct HTTP returned 403; metadata states that method, without a fabricated HTML hash. An inconsistent AHRQ STAR graphic/prose expansion was not used.
