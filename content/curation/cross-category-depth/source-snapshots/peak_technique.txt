L1151@P28: GOALS OF ASTHMA SELF-MANAGEMENT EDUCATION AND KEY EDUCATIONAL
L1152@P28: MESSAGES
L1153@P28: Patient education is an essential component of successful asthma management. Current
L1154@P28: management approaches require patients and families to effectively carry out complex
L1155@P28: pharmacologic regimens, institute environmental control strategies, detect and self-treat most
L1156@P28: asthma exacerbations, and communicate appropriately with health care providers. Patient
L1157@P28: education is the mechanism through which patients learn to accomplish those tasks
L1158@P28: successfully. It is also a powerful tool for helping patients gain the motivation, skill, and
L1159@P28: confidence to control their asthma (Butz et al. 2005; Gibson et al. 2000; Guevara et al. 2003;
L1160@P28: Levy et al. 2000; Perneger et al. 2002). Research shows that asthma education can be
L1161@P28: cost-effective and can reduce morbidity for both adults and children, especially among high-risk
L1162@P28: patients (Gallefoss and Bakke 2001; Gibson et al. 2000, 2003; Guevara et al. 2003; Schermer
L1163@P28: et al. 2002; Sullivan et al. 2002).

L1168@P29: FIGURE 3–11. HOW TO USE YOUR PEAK FLOW METER
L1169@P29: A peak flow meter is a device that measures how
L1170@P29: well air moves out of your lungs. During an
L1171@P29: asthma episode, the airways of the lungs usually
L1172@P29: begin to narrow slowly. The peak flow meter may
L1173@P29: tell you if there is narrowing in the airways
L1174@P29: hours—sometimes even days—before you have
L1175@P29: any asthma symptoms.
L1176@P29: By taking your medicine(s) early (before
L1177@P29: symptoms), you may be able to stop the episode
L1178@P29: quickly and avoid a severe asthma episode.
L1179@P29: Peak flow meters are used to check your asthma
L1180@P29: the way that blood pressure cuffs are used to
L1181@P29: check high blood pressure.
L1182@P29: The peak flow meter also can be used to help you
L1183@P29: and your doctor:
L1184@P29:  Learn what makes your asthma worse.
L1185@P29:  Decide if your treatment plan is working well.
L1186@P29:  Decide when to add or stop medicine.
L1187@P29:  Decide when to seek emergency care.
L1188@P29: A peak flow meter is most helpful for patients who
L1189@P29: must take asthma medicine daily. Patients age 5
L1190@P29: and older are usually able to use a peak flow
L1191@P29: meter. Ask your doctor or nurse to show you how
L1192@P29: to use a peak flow meter.
L1193@P29: How To Use Your Peak Flow Meter
L1194@P29:  Do the following five steps with your peak
L1195@P29: flow meter:
L1196@P29: 1. Move the indicator to the bottom of the
L1197@P29: numbered scale.
L1198@P29: 2. Stand up.

L1200@P29: completely.

L1202@P29: close your lips around it. Do not put your

L1212@P29: Find Your Personal Best Peak Flow
L1213@P29: Number
L1214@P29: Your personal best peak flow number is the
L1215@P29: highest peak flow number you can achieve over a
L1216@P29: 2-week period when your asthma is under good
L1217@P29: control. Good control is when you feel good and
L1218@P29: do not have any asthma symptoms.
L1219@P29: Each patient’s asthma is different, and your best
L1220@P29: peak flow may be higher or lower than the peak
L1221@P29: flow of someone of your same height, weight, and
L1222@P29: sex. This means that it is important for you to find
L1223@P29: your own personal best peak flow number. Your
L1224@P29: treatment plan needs to be based on your own
L1225@P29: personal best peak flow number.
L1226@P29: To find out your personal best peak flow number,
L1227@P29: take peak flow readings:
L1228@P29:  At least twice a day for 2 to 3 weeks.
L1229@P29:  When you wake up and in late afternoon or
L1230@P29: early evening.
L1231@P29:  15–20 minutes after you take your inhaled
L1232@P29: short-acting beta2-agonist for quick relief.
L1233@P29-30:  As instructed by your doctor. Section 3, Component 2: Education for a Partnership in Asthma Care

L1293@P31: Establish and Maintain a Partnership
L1294@P31: The Expert Panel recommends that a
L1295@P31: partnership between patient and clinician be
L1296@P31: established to promote effective asthma
L1297@P31: management (Evidence A).

L1299@P31: promote open communication and ensure that

L1303@P31: disease objectively and take medication
L1304@P31: effectively (Clark et al. 1995, 1998, 2000; Evans
L1305@P31: et al. 1997; Love et al. 2000; Marabini et al.
