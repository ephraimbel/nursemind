# Infection, renal and cardiac source-fidelity review — September 17, 2026

Eleven adult nursing references add 69 sections and 138 individually cited blocks, totaling 4,440 body words across 16 primary CDC/NIDDK/NHLBI source pages. The manifest maps every block to its source. This batch is complementary to the separately reserved C. difficile, Candida auris and norovirus guides; those three drafts were excluded before integration.

## Source evidence and reuse

The original federal prose was read through primary-source pages and direct HTTP retrieval. The `source-snapshots` directory retains extracted source text and metadata for all 16 used pages. Each metadata record contains the requested/resolved URL, retrieval date, a hash of the fetched HTML and a hash of the retained extracted text. Raw HTML is not retained. Hashes of the saved text were verified before integration. Source publication/update dates are recorded when available; a 2026 retrieval date does not turn an older source into a new guideline.

The adaptation uses public-domain federal prose under the project's sourcing policy. It excludes images, videos, third-party linked content, externally attributed algorithms and medication tables. The snapshots are supporting evidence for review, not content to display verbatim. Original NurseMind wording organizes the sources around observation, preparation, reporting, follow-up and education. Workflow prompts are editorial synthesis of the cited source; they are not presented as independently validated clinical protocols.

## Source-specific checks

- **CRE:** CDC's December 17, 2025 guidance supports resistance-mechanism awareness, acute-care versus nursing-home precautions, prompt laboratory alerts, transfer communication and screening/pre-emptive isolation after relevant overseas healthcare within six months. The guide does not choose an antibiotic or infer susceptibility from an organism label.
- **Hemodialysis:** NIDDK supports access identification, daily observation, absent vibration reporting, clot/scar-related flow problems, treatment intolerance and bleeding from disconnection. The entry does not reproduce cannulation or emergency clamping instructions, a fixed dialysis prescription, or a claim that machine alarms reliably detect every dislodgement.
- **Peritoneal dialysis:** CDC's July 10, 2026 provider page supports cloudy effluent, abdominal symptoms, exit-site changes, aseptic technique and prompt evaluation. NIDDK supports supply checks, exchange preparation and immediate reporting of infection symptoms. No universal antibiotic, specimen protocol or dialysate prescription is supplied.
- **Kidney biopsy:** NIDDK supports the medication/bleeding-risk review, preparation, observation and postprocedure warning signs. Its two-week strenuous-activity interval is attributed and remains subordinate to the individual procedural instructions. No universal anticoagulant hold or restart interval is supplied.
- **Nephrotic syndrome:** NIDDK supports the syndrome, possible causes, protein testing, edema, infection/clot/kidney risks and cause-specific follow-up. The older source's simplified anticoagulation rule and broad vaccination wording are not reproduced as universal instructions.
- **Kidney stones:** NIDDK supports assessment, variable treatment approaches, stone analysis, recurrence evaluation and individualized fluids. The source medication table, fixed fluid targets and unsourced forced-hydration instructions are excluded. No drug is recommended from the table's problematic class labels.
- **Cardiac catheterization:** Separate NHLBI preparation, procedure and recovery pages support history/medication review, monitoring, access-site and limb warning signs, and discharge planning. The correct preparation URL is `/preparing`; the nonexistent `/before` URL is not retained. No universal fasting, bed-rest or medication-hold interval is invented.
- **Planned cardioversion:** Only the cardioversion portion of NHLBI's procedures page is used. Electrode positioning retains “possibly the back.” Its imprecise emergency defibrillation wording is excluded. No shock-energy algorithm, medication regimen, transesophageal-echo rule or universal anticoagulation duration is inferred.
- **Permanent pacemaker:** NHLBI supports early checks, individualized recovery, ID cards, follow-up, interference precautions and symptom reporting. The six-inch phone/headphone separation is attributed. Device-specific procedure assessment replaces a blanket MRI prohibition. Fixed battery/recovery timelines and the source's fluid/pericarditis wording are excluded.
- **ICD:** NHLBI supports contacting the provider for any shock, emergency evaluation for repeated strong shocks or heart-attack/cardiac-arrest signs, follow-up, precautions, emotional support and clinician-managed goals-of-care discussions. No self-deactivation technique, wearable-device operation, fixed battery life or driving-law rule is supplied.
- **Heart surgery:** NHLBI supports the individualized hospital/discharge plan, recovery concerns, medicines, follow-up and rehabilitation. No universal sternal restrictions, dressing protocol or numerical symptom threshold is inferred.
