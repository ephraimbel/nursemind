# Cross-specialty library depth — September 17, 2026

Added **14 practical nursing references**, with **56 sections, 114 individually cited guidance points and 17 direct federal source pages**. This pass expands daily teaching, function and recovery across cardiology, endocrinology, gastroenterology, eye/hearing care, rheumatology, orthopedics, dermatology, neurology, behavioral health and respiratory care.

The additions were compared with the registered catalog and the earlier bedside, oncology/community, dementia/kidney and specialty-support passes. They add practical teaching depth alongside the existing diagnoses and procedures. No existing entry was removed, and none of the new IDs or normalized titles duplicated the pre-integration catalog. The existing diabetes foot-care, heart-failure discharge, low-vision and menopause pages were retained instead of creating overlapping general guides.

## Source and scope checks

Every new guidance bullet has a citation ID resolving to an HTTPS source declared on its own entry. Source records include agency, page title, section locator, original review/update date and retrieval date. `lastSourceFidelityReview` records comparison with the cited source; it does not assert independent RN review, a guideline update or endorsement by a federal agency.

Only the original government health-education prose was adapted. No source images, videos, handout layouts, third-party curricula, copyrighted scales, linked drug monographs or MedlinePlus encyclopedia pages were imported. NEI’s requested courtesy credit and NHLBI’s requested source credit appear in the relevant source records.

Source-specific limits:

- CGM guidance is for everyday patient education, with device-specific instructions and backup measurement. It does not authorize inpatient dosing from sensor values or introduce universal alert targets, sensor lifetimes or model eligibility rules.
- Home blood-pressure preparation and measurement intervals come from the CDC page. No universal treatment target or medicine-adjustment rule was added.
- Asthma content explains use of an existing clinician-authored plan. It excludes the older page’s reliever-only treatment language and does not replace the existing medication-selection reference.
- Hip precautions, weight bearing, activity progression and medicine holds remain individualized; no universal postoperative timeline was added.
- Eczema guidance excludes blanket vaccine claims and DIY bleach-bath or wet-wrap recipes. The two older eczema entries previously offered generic bleach-bath instructions; those individual bullets now require clinician-specific instructions and cite the direct NIAMS page. Their other content and historical review dates were not relabeled as newly reviewed.
- No patient-specific dose, emergency drug algorithm, calculator exposure, AI provider change or PHI workflow was added.
