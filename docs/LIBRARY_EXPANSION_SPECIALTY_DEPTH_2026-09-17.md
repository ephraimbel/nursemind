## Targeted corrections to existing content

Five older entries received narrowly scoped corrections, supported by three additional source pages:

- **Pulmonary hypertension diagnosis and classification:** corrected the resting mPAP boundary and pre-capillary PVR boundary; clarified that pre-capillary measurements alone do not establish Group 1 PAH. The corrected classification definition is attributed prose instead of an uncited table row. Removed an inaccurate AMBITION/COPD trial statement and added source-backed context distinguishing ILD-associated from COPD-associated PH.
- **Cor pulmonale:** replaced blanket restriction of selected therapies to Group 1 with specialist-selection context for PH due to lung disease.
- **Bronchiectasis and cor pulmonale:** removed wording implying that influenza, pneumococcal, COVID and pertussis vaccines all follow an annual schedule; directs readers to current age/risk, prior-dose, interval and precaution guidance.
- **Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis:** replaced the unsupported implication that treating reflux slows lung progression. The replacement preserves the conditional recommendation and very low evidence quality, and distinguishes respiratory outcomes from treatment of symptomatic GERD.

Older whole-entry source-review dates remain unchanged because this was not a full review of those entries. Their remaining medication, diagnostic and outcome statements still need the broader source-quality and credentialed clinical review already recorded in the Library audit.

## Source fidelity and clinical review

The [review manifest](../content/curation/specialty-support-depth/manifest.json) maps every new guidance point to its source and records independent clinical review as pending, with no reviewer or approval date. Six new guides are conservatively proposed for Tier A review and four for Tier B; the tier assignments also require clinical confirmation. The five corrected legacy entries remain pending Tier A review. The manifest is a review record, not an automated publication gate.

Source comparison on September 17, 2026 does not mean independent RN review or that the original source was revised that day. Original update dates and section locators are retained in each citation. All new entries are local development content; no production publication occurred.

Source decisions and exclusions:

- Original federal web prose is adapted with agency attribution. [NHLBI policy](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/about/contact/trademark-branding-and-logo) and [NIDCD policy](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/policies) were checked. No agency logos, images, videos, formatted handouts, figures or third-party materials were imported.
- ATS and ESC facts are paraphrased under `factCitationOnly`. The ATS article is not treated as commercially reusable merely because it is hosted in PMC. No society text, tables or algorithms were reproduced.
- Bronchiectasis material excludes medication regimens, new-drug approval claims, fixed antibiotic durations and head-down positioning instructions.
- Pulmonary fibrosis and PH guides do not provide oxygen titration, acute treatment or pregnancy-management protocols.
- Sickle-cell content preserves urgent recognition thresholds and individual pain planning; it excludes analgesic selection, pediatric spleen palpation instructions and pregnancy medication advice.
- Bleeding-disorder guidance excludes factor doses and perioperative treatment orders. Thalassemia content excludes fixed transfusion/lab intervals, chelator selection and outdated claims about the only curative treatment.
- Balance guidance starts with CDC emergency recognition. Tinnitus guidance starts with NIDCD sudden-hearing-loss urgency; neither guide teaches diagnostic maneuvers or medication treatment.
- Hidradenitis content excludes approved-biologic counts and bleach dilution recipes.
