# Oncology, community and workforce references — September 17, 2026

Added **26 references**: 12 oncology supportive-care guides, 9 community/home-care guides and 5 nursing workplace-health guides. They contain **78 sections and 181 individually cited bullets**, with 34 specific source records from NCI, CDC/NIOSH and FDA.

Current combined catalog: **1,770 entries / 310 general references**. The side-conversation baseline was 1,741; the concurrent chat added three lab/care-plan references before this batch was registered. This batch itself adds 26, removes none, and has no new ID or normalized-title collisions.

## Organization and integration

- New **Community & home care** collection under Specialty nursing: all nine new community references plus existing home-fall prevention, discharge teaching and health-literacy references.
- New **Nursing workplace health** collection under Bedside nursing: all five workforce references plus existing PPE, mechanical-lift and de-escalation references.
- Oncology gains the 12 supportive-care references and food-safety reference. Older adult care gains heat and home-device preparedness.
- Added 42 curated related-entry edges. Every new reference has a topic home and a working related-entry path.
- Total topic coverage is now **31 collections / 375 memberships / 305 distinct entries**; 1,465 entries remain outside these selected collections but are available in search and A–Z.
- Existing concurrent lab, reader, care-plan and organization changes were retained. This batch uses its own three content files, links file and test file.

## Adjacent food-safety correction

The existing neutropenic-precautions reference contained a blanket prohibition on all raw produce. Its two food-safety bullets now describe thorough produce washing, properly cooked animal foods, pasteurized dairy and the individual oncology plan. The new primary sources are [NCI infection/neutropenia guidance](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/side-effects/infection) and [CDC safer food choices](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/weakened-immune-systems.html). The flower precaution was separated from the food statement.

The added source details explicitly support only those food-safety bullets. The entry’s older whole-entry review date was preserved; its other high-risk clinical content was not re-reviewed or endorsed by this correction.
