# App Privacy — pre-filled nutrition label answers

Apple asks ~30 yes/no/list questions when you fill out the **App Privacy** section in App Store Connect. Below is the complete walkthrough, derived from the locked architecture. Copy each answer into the corresponding ASC field.

**Architectural ground truth:**
- Anonymous-by-default Supabase account on first launch (UUID, no identity)
- Optional Sign in with Apple linkage (Apple-supplied identifier; email if user shares)
- Apple-handled billing (StoreKit), entitlement managed by RevenueCat
- Server-side PHI scrubber rejects patient-identifying inputs before any model call
- Service providers and advertising partners enumerated in the updated `/privacy` source: Apple, Supabase, RevenueCat, Meta, TikTok, Anthropic, OpenAI, Sentry, PostHog, Vercel. Publish the policy update before releasing the Meta-enabled app.
- Meta and TikTok attribution SDKs; initialization, identification, and advertising events require authorized ATT consent. TikTok automatic payment/enhanced-data tracking is disabled; verified paid transactions use Purchase, and free trials use StartTrial with zero revenue.
- US-only at v1 launch
- Architecturally PHI-free — no name, MRN, DOB, or chart fields

**Used to track you:** User ID, Device ID, Purchase History, and Product Interaction. Update App Store Connect before releasing the Meta-enabled build. This document does not change the live label.

## Data Linked to You

When ASC asks "Do you collect this data?" answer **Yes** for the items below; mark each as **Linked to the user's identity** and select the listed purposes. The identifiers, purchases, and app-activation data listed in the Tracking section are used for **Tracking** when permission is granted; contact and clinical content are not.

### Contact Info

| Type | Collect? | Linked? | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | Yes | App Functionality |
| **Email Address** | Yes | Yes | App Functionality |
| Phone Number | No | — | — |
| Physical Address | No | — | — |
| Other User Contact Info | No | — | — |

*Notes:*
- *Name:* Optional **display name** in Profile — used for the in-app greeting and Profile header. The user controls whether to enter anything; it can be a nickname, initials, or left blank. We don't ask for legal name.
- *Email:* Only collected if the user opts to sign in with Apple AND chooses to share email. Otherwise the account is fully anonymous. Used for cross-device sync recovery and to receive customer support replies.

### User Content

| Type | Collect? | Linked? | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emails or Text Messages | No | — | — |
| Photos or Videos | No | — | — |
| Audio Data | No | — | — |
| Gameplay Content | No | — | — |
| **Customer Support** | Yes | Yes | App Functionality |
| **Other User Content** | Yes | Yes | App Functionality |

*Notes:*
- *Audio Data:* Voice input uses on-device `SFSpeechRecognizer`. Audio never leaves the device — only the transcribed text is sent to the AI co-pilot for processing.
- *Customer Support:* Email content the user sends to `hello@nursemind.app`.
- *Other User Content:* The user's saved bookmarks, profile preferences (role/unit/years), and AI conversation history. Stored in our Supabase Postgres database for cross-device sync. Conversation history is retained for up to 90 days for quota enforcement and abuse review.

### Identifiers

| Type | Collect? | Linked? | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **User ID** | Yes | Yes | App Functionality, Analytics, Developer’s Advertising or Marketing, Third-Party Advertising |
| **Device ID** | Yes | Yes | Analytics, App Functionality, Developer’s Advertising or Marketing, Third-Party Advertising |

*Notes:*
- *User ID:* The anonymous UUID generated by Supabase Auth on first launch. If the user opts to sign in with Apple, this UUID is preserved and aliased to the Apple identifier on the backend.

### Purchases

| Type | Collect? | Linked? | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Purchase History** | Yes | Yes | App Functionality, Analytics, Developer’s Advertising or Marketing, Third-Party Advertising |

*Notes:*
- We receive subscription entitlement state (active / inactive / trial / monthly / yearly) from Apple via RevenueCat. We do not see the user's payment card, billing address, or Apple ID password. Used to gate Pro features and, with consent, measure advertising through RevenueCat and Meta.

## Device, usage, and diagnostic data

Advertising identifiers and activation events can be linked by advertising partners. Classify them as linked; diagnostic categories below remain not linked.

### Identifiers

| Type | Collect? | Linked? | Purposes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Device ID** | Yes | Yes | Analytics, App Functionality, Developer’s Advertising or Marketing, Third-Party Advertising |

*Notes:*
- Sentry uses a per-install device identifier in crash reports. PostHog uses a per-install distinct ID for product analytics. Meta and RevenueCat additionally associate attribution identifiers with subscription events after consent; the combined category is linked.

## Tracking

**Q: Do you or your third-party partners use data from this app to track users?**
**A: Yes.**

With ATT consent, Meta receives advertising/app identifiers, app activations, and RevenueCat trial/subscription events. TikTok also measures installs and subscriptions. Mark **User ID, Device ID, Purchase History, and Product Interaction** as used for tracking and linked, with Analytics, Developer’s Advertising or Marketing, and Third-Party Advertising purposes in addition to the existing app-functionality purposes. Clinical questions, answers, library activity, names, emails, and phone numbers are not provided to Meta or TikTok.

Keep the RevenueCat Meta option to send events without authorized ATT **off**. Do not configure email or phone matching attributes. Disable automatic/codeless event logging in Meta; RevenueCat alone sends subscription events.

## What changes if you add features later

Track these as future Privacy-label updates if you ship them:

- **Push notifications via APNs** — no new data category (uses anonymous device token)
- **Sign in with Google** (if added in v1.1) — same email handling as Sign in with Apple, no change to label
- **HealthKit integration** (v2+) — would add Health & Fitness category, which is a major label change
- **In-app survey or NPS prompt** — adds User Content → Customer Support if responses are stored
- **In-app ad display** — review Advertising Data and additional SDK disclosures; acquisition attribution already requires tracking disclosures.

When in doubt, re-read this doc before filling out the App Privacy form for any future release.
