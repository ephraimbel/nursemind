# Recent clinical evidence expansion

Added 14 review drafts across Drugs, Labs and Reference, with 3,663 body words, 91 cited blocks and 39 source records. All connect to existing collections and related entries. The review-build registry contains 2,161 entries; the ordinary registry remains at 2,147 because this batch is excluded pending independent clinical review.

The medication additions are suzetrigine, gepotidacin, brensocatib, nerandomilast, donanemab and zuranolone. The diagnostic additions cover plasma p-tau217/amyloid ratio testing and home vaginal HPV collection. Six guides address HIV prevention, adult RSV vaccination, obesity medication comparisons, diabetic kidney combination therapy, oral semaglutide cardiovascular outcomes and blood-pressure targets in diabetes.

Recent evidence includes the [August 2026 DAN-RSV follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42666088/), [2026 SOUL secondary analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41627802/), [2026 obesity-treatment cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41891334/), [2026 blood-pressure review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/endocrinology/articles/10.3389/fendo.2026.1850865/full) and [2026 home HPV guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12994907/). Current manufacturer labels were checked separately from trial dates. Older pivotal trials and stable laboratory definitions retain their actual publication dates.

The entries explicitly preserve material limitations: an uncertain second-season vaccine estimate, a nonsignificant overall heart-failure outcome despite a favorable subgroup, observational rather than randomized cardiovascular comparisons, albuminuria rather than kidney-failure endpoints, and device-specific diagnostic performance. The drug summaries include safety considerations but omit dosing and unsupported nursing content; they do not replace complete labels.

Source records and the independent review queue are in [the curation packet](../content/curation/recent-evidence/README.md). Clinical content, source rights, warnings and label completeness still require review. The `DEBUG && RECENT_EVIDENCE_REVIEW` boundary excludes entries, topic memberships and related links from ordinary and Release builds, including when only the review flag is set. No production index or backend deployment was performed for this batch.

Software validation passed for the initial 24-test content/navigation run and the ordinary-build exclusion check. After the HPV classification correction, all five batch tests passed again and the final compiled export matched the manifest. A separate compiler check verified the Release boundary. The [compiled audit](library-audit/2026-09-19-recent-evidence/summary.md) compares the complete Swift records against the editorial JSON, including citation records and collection memberships. It also keeps inherited source debt visible: 752 existing entries have uncited blocks, and 1,059 have source-locator issues.

The review app built and was inspected on the dedicated content simulator. Inspection covered the Alzheimer biomarker context reader, its live related link to donanemab, warning-section navigation, the exact DailyMed citation URL, HPV follow-up context and the 2026 RSV study section. The lab reader now omits empty numerical sections for these diagnostic guides. The HPV guide’s priority concept was corrected after visual inspection so it appears under infection instead of hematology. The final build passed, the corrected header was visually confirmed, and the existing potassium reference-range reader was checked for regression.

The remaining highest-value breadth gaps identified in the title manifest are dedicated diagnosis overviews for uncomplicated type 2 diabetes, hypertension and obesity. Repairing inherited uncited claims is also necessary before claiming the whole library is evidence-backed. This expansion does not establish a complete medical index or exhaustive coverage of the latest literature.
