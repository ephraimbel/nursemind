## Source fidelity and limits

Each content bullet declares a citation that resolves on that entry. Source records include the original page title, agency, section locator, source date where available, retrieval date and reuse classification. September 17, 2026 is the source-comparison date, not a claim of independent RN review or a new clinical guideline revision.

Nineteen pages use original federal health-education prose, adapted with agency attribution. The VA fatigue article and AASLD/IDSA monitoring guidance use conservative `factCitationOnly` attribution: clinical facts are paraphrased, without importing source text, tables or algorithms. No National MS Society handouts, licensed drug monographs, medical encyclopedia content, copyrighted scales, figures or videos were imported.

Specific source decisions:

- Sepsis content covers recovery and prompt reassessment for an infection that is worsening or not improving. It does not add an acute resuscitation or antibiotic protocol.
- MS fatigue includes sleep, medicines and other possible contributors; it does not attribute every change automatically to MS or introduce a drug regimen.
- Lupus pregnancy planning remains with the treating team. The Sjögren’s guide avoids eyelid-cleanser recipes and individual eye-drop selection.
- GERD includes symptoms needing assessment. IBD nutrition preserves individual food tolerance and condition-specific absorption differences without prescribing a universal restriction diet.
- HBV content excludes age-based vaccine schedules, infant prophylaxis algorithms and universal monitoring intervals.
- HCV cure confirmation is explicitly tied to testing after treatment completion, using AASLD/IDSA guidance. The guide acknowledges selected earlier assessment without offering an independent eligibility algorithm. The CDC page’s abbreviated timing wording was not reused.
- Latent-TB teaching distinguishes inactive infection from active disease. CDC’s adverse-reaction instructions and need for clinical monitoring are retained, while drug doses, regimen tables and lab-based stopping thresholds are excluded.
- HIV content uses the actual prescribed regimen. It does not assume everyone uses daily tablets or apply a generic missed-dose rule to long-acting injections.
- Interstitial-cystitis guidance preserves NIDDK’s distinction between relaxing a tight pelvic floor and unsupervised strengthening; it does not substitute a generic Kegel routine or fixed fluid target.
