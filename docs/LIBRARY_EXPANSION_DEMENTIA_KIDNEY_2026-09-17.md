### Clinical boundaries checked

- Dementia guides preserve the person’s participation and dignity. They distinguish longstanding sleep patterns from sudden changes needing assessment, and mealtime assistance from symptoms requiring a swallowing assessment. No blanket texture or fluid-thickening instructions were added.
- Wandering guidance covers supervision, identification, exit alerts and supported outings. Instructions to confine a person or lock them alone in a home were omitted.
- Kidney medication teaching uses an individualized illness and restart plan. It does not supply a universal hold list or recommend stopping kidney-protective medicines independently.
- Transplant care emphasizes prescribed medicines, surveillance even when well, infection reporting and practical support. No dose changes, numerical fever thresholds, transplant-eligibility rules or historical insurance coverage promises were imported.
- Continence guidance distinguishes incomplete emptying from apparent urine output, calls for immediate assessment of inability to void, and explains postvoid residual without creating a catheterization threshold.
- Fecal continence covers history, diaries, skin care and clinician-directed options. OTC regimens and outdated device/surgery details were omitted.
- Care-service guidance distinguishes personal care, skilled home health, meal delivery, adult day services and respite. It does not promise coverage or eligibility.
