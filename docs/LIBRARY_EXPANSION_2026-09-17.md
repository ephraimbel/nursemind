# Bedside library expansion — September 17, 2026

Added 23 source-backed references after checking the titles and relevant bodies in the existing 1,718-entry catalog. The registered catalog now contains 1,741 entries, including 281 general references. This is a focused expansion of adult bedside assessment, daily care and communication; entry counts do not establish clinical completeness.

The additions contain approximately 3,385 words across three source files. Every content block has entry-local citations. All 23 entries are searchable, assigned to existing topic collections and connected to related content. Added 38 related-entry edges and 33 topic memberships across 12 collections. The 29 existing collections now have 337 memberships covering 275 distinct entries; 1,466 catalog entries remain outside those selected collections.

## Corrections in existing content

The checked SHEA/IDSA/APIC guidance supports daily toothbrushing without routine oral chlorhexidine for adult VAP prevention. Corrected conflicting wording in `MechanicalVentilationSample`, `PneumoniaSample` and `VAPSample`, adding the direct primary citation. Removed the outdated bundled prevention sentence from the VAP definition and separated stress-ulcer prophylaxis from VAP prevention. The aspiration-pneumonia entry now cites CDC for its general oral-care statement. Removed the unsupported daily oral-chlorhexidine component from the central-line prevention list.

Skin antisepsis and chlorhexidine bathing references were not changed by this oral-care correction. The existing entries' older whole-entry source-fidelity dates remain unchanged because their other claims were not comprehensively reviewed in this pass.
