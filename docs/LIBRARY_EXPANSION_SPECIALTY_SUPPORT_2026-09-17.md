## Content decisions

- Hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis have separate explanations, access/infection observations and support needs. Dialysis nutrition explicitly distinguishes their potassium and fluid plans.
- PD infection observations use the CDC page dated July 10, 2026. NIDDK material supplies background and routine planning; its bag-warming directions, fixed exchange schedules and solution strengths were not imported.
- CPAP guidance is for ongoing sleep-apnea support. Fit, cleaning, comfort, adherence and reassessment are separated from acute NIV initiation. Manufacturer instructions control equipment cleaning; FDA source supports the distinction between cleaning and add-on bacterial reduction.
- Oxygen guidance covers home fire safety and pulse-oximeter limitations without creating an oxygen-titration protocol. The former FDA oxygen-concentrator consumer URL now redirects to Pulse Oximeter Basics; the citation uses the actual destination and supports only oximetry claims.
- Urinary diversion distinguishes an external conduit pouch from a catheterized reservoir and neobladder. No generic catheterization or irrigation schedule is supplied. Bowel-ostomy support adds hydration, food tolerance, skin/pouch concerns and recovery context to the existing pouch-change procedure.
- Dysphagia screening is distinguished from diagnosis. Texture changes, posture and swallowing maneuvers remain individualized. The older NIDCD fact sheet is used for stable mechanisms and rehabilitation concepts, paired with NLM-authored test information; it is not an acute aspiration protocol.
- Stroke support includes communication, continence, swallowing, emotional adjustment and daily function. Conservative kidney care covers informed choices and ongoing symptom support without predicting survival.
